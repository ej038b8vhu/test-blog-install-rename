# Finding Inspiration on a Walk

A short walk offers a change of pace and a chance to notice details you might otherwise miss: the shape of a building, the sound of rain, or the way light falls across a street.

You do not need to turn every walk into a productivity exercise. Sometimes paying attention is enough.
