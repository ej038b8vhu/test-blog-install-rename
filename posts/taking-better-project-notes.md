# Taking Better Project Notes

Useful project notes capture more than what happened. Record the decision, why it was made, and what should happen next.

A simple structure is often enough:

- **Context:** What were you working on?
- **Decision:** What did you choose?
- **Next step:** What needs to happen now?

These details make it easier to return to a project after a break.
